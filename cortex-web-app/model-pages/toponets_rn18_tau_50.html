About This Model

TopoNet ResNet-18 with τ=50. The weakest topographic constraint in the τ sweep, essentially functioning as supervised ResNet-18 with minimal spatial organization pressure.

When evaluated against fMRI recordings from human participants, TopoNet RN18 (τ=50) achieves a moderate global Pearson correlation of 0.315 (averaged across all evaluation datasets and brain regions). Its strongest predictions are in the PPA (Scene) area.
