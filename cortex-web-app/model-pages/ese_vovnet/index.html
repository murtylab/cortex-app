About This Model

ESE-VoVNet uses One-Shot Aggregation (OSA) to reduce redundancy in DenseNet's dense connections, combined with Effective Squeeze-Excitation (eSE) blocks for channel recalibration. It is designed for real-time inference on detection tasks.

When evaluated against fMRI recordings from human participants, ESE-VoVNet achieves a moderate global Pearson correlation of 0.342 (averaged across all evaluation datasets and brain regions). Its strongest predictions are in the PPA (Scene) area.
